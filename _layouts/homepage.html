What ships in the starter

Checkout with server-side totals

Supporters build a cart in the browser. The Worker rebuilds prices from trusted campaign data, then Stripe saves the payment method on-site so you can charge only if the campaign succeeds. Multi-campaign carts are supported.

Review the pledge worker

Campaign states and charge timing

The starter handles upcoming, live, and post-campaign states, deadline timing in your configured timezone, launch reminder signups, stretch goals, and batched settlement after a campaign closes.

Read about The Pool

Rewards, add-ons, tax, and shipping

You get reward tiers, campaign add-ons, platform merch, inventory checks, configurable tax providers, USPS quotes, fallback shipping rates, and tip totals that stay aligned in checkout, email, and reports.

See merch and add-ons

Supporter tools after checkout

Backers manage pledges from email magic links. Campaigns can publish diary updates, announcements, community decisions, share links, and runner-ready pledge and fulfillment reports.

Explore campaign workflows

Admin tools for a forked site

A role-scoped admin dashboard lets teams edit settings, campaigns, add-ons, content, users, reports, and media while structured config and design tokens handle normal rebranding work.

Open customization docs

Launch and maintenance basics

The starter includes lazy cart loading, responsive media, deferred video embeds, asset minification, SEO metadata, accessibility baselines, security hardening, Playwright coverage, and ops docs.

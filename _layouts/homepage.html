What The Pool does

It gives you campaign pages, checkout, admin tools, emails, and reports without handing your project to a closed crowdfunding platform.

Free and yours to run

What is included today

Pledge checkout that protects the goal

Contact and address fields stay editable while payment loads. Tax and shipping refresh when the destination changes. Each campaign in a shared checkout becomes a separate pledge, charged only after it ends successfully.

Review checkout and payments

Campaign timing and outcomes

The Pool handles upcoming, live, and finished campaigns, deadline timing in your timezone, launch reminder signups, stretch goals, and batched charges after a successful campaign closes.

Read about The Pool

Rewards, add-ons, tax, and shipping

Offer reward tiers, add-ons, inventory limits, optional tips, USPS quotes, and fallback shipping rates. Tax estimates show when local coverage may be missing.

Supporter follow-up

Backers manage pledges through secure email links. Diary emails link to the individual update. Delivery retries preserve saved pledges, and campaign updates include an unsubscribe option.

What The Pool does

It gives you campaign pages, checkout, admin tools, emails, and reports without handing your project to a closed crowdfunding platform.

What is included today

Pledge checkout that protects the goal

Supporters build a cart on your site. A small Cloudflare backend recalculates totals from trusted campaign data, then Stripe saves the payment method. One checkout can include several campaigns, and each campaign is charged only if it succeeds.

Review the pledge worker

Campaign timing and outcomes

The Pool handles upcoming, live, and finished campaigns, deadline timing in your timezone, launch reminder signups, stretch goals, and batched charges after a successful campaign closes.
